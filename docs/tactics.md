# Empirical Alpha Tactics

Based on the quantitative analysis of Polymarket user behavior and the structural biases observed:

1. **Short the Default ("Yes" Bias):** Retail users systematically overtrade the default option (usually "Yes"). The empirical edge is found by taking the other side of this psychological bias (betting "No").
2. **Favor the Favorites:** The paper finds no general longshot bias. Profitable traders lean heavily into high-probability favorites because retail consistently overpays for middling longshots.
3. **Early Information Arbitrage:** Enter markets earlier in their lifecycle when mispricings are common and information is still being incorporated.
4. **Late Resolution Speed:** Close to settlement, exploit stale orders by reacting to new information faster than retail bettors can cancel or update them.
5. **Extreme Longshot Sniping:** Occasionally buy extreme longshots only when they are fundamentally mispriced due to rare events, not random gambling.
