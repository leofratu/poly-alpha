What we made public for startup verification

A short overview of the public website details Poly-Alpha exposes so reviewers can validate the business, product, team, and contact path.

Startup program reviews need a public website that clearly explains the business, product, team, and contact path. Poly-Alpha's public site is structured to make those details easy to validate without requiring a login.

Business identity

The site names Georgy Bitaev as founder and publishes the business-domain contact email georgy@polyaialpha.online. This gives reviewers a direct, domain-matching way to contact the company.

Digital-native product

Poly-Alpha is presented as SaaS market-intelligence infrastructure for prediction-market research workflows. The product pages describe the workspace, signal movement graph, research notes, private beta, and onboarding path.

Public pages

The site includes Product, Pricing, Docs, Team, Blog, Contact, Privacy, Terms, and Security pages. Each page is public and reachable through the main navigation or footer.

Claims discipline

The site avoids fake customer logos, unverifiable traction numbers, fake API keys, and exaggerated claims. The content focuses on what is currently true: founder-led private beta, SaaS direction, and reviewable market-intelligence workflows.
