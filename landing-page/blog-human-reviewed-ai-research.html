Designing human-reviewed AI research loops

AI can help summarize and structure market context, but Poly-Alpha keeps final interpretation and decisions under human control.

AI systems are useful when they reduce research friction without hiding the reasoning process. For market intelligence, this means AI should help gather context, organize drivers, draft summaries, and flag questions for review.

Why human review stays central

Prediction-market research often combines quantitative movement with qualitative context. A model may identify a change, but a human reviewer still needs to decide whether the change is meaningful, relevant, and appropriate for the team's use case.

The loop Poly-Alpha supports

The intended loop is simple: monitor signals, identify movement, collect source context, draft a research brief, and review the brief before action. Each step should leave behind a readable record.

What the product avoids

Poly-Alpha avoids black-box claims, fake automation promises, and unreviewed execution language. The product is positioned as AI-assisted analytics infrastructure, not as an autonomous trading system.

Private beta focus

During private beta, Poly-Alpha is focused on learning from teams that already work with market data and need a clearer way to turn signals into documented research outputs.
