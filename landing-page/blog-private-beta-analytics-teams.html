Opening pilot conversations with analytics teams

Poly-Alpha is speaking with teams that need cleaner workflows for prediction-market data, source context, and reviewable briefs.

Poly-Alpha is currently founder-led and private beta oriented. That is intentional. The product is still being shaped around real workflows, and the best feedback comes from teams with concrete research needs.

Who the beta is for

The beta is designed for research, analytics, and product teams that want to monitor public prediction-market signals, understand market movement, and build internal notes or dashboards around those signals.

What a pilot includes

A pilot conversation covers the team's use case, target markets, workflow requirements, security expectations, and whether a simple workspace or scoped integration path makes sense.

What is public today

The public site provides the product overview, pricing intent, documentation guide, team identity, contact path, privacy policy, terms, and security overview. This is meant to make the company easy to validate before a conversation starts.
